Delete your MsgReminder account

Use this page to permanently delete your MsgReminder account and related reminder data. This meets Google Play’s account deletion requirement and works even if the app is not installed.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, mobile)
  • All reminders and attached photos
  • Password reset tokens and login access

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. After deletion you can create a new account with the same email if you want.

Signed-in users can also delete from the MsgReminder app: Settings → Delete account.
